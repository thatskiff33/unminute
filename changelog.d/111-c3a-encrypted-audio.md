Security hardening (#111, phase C):

- **Meeting audio is now encrypted on this computer.** The app's working copy of each meeting's audio is encrypted with AES-256-GCM under that meeting's own key, which Windows protects for your account, and no unencrypted copy is written. It is checked in full once when it is imported; a part that has been damaged or changed stops transcription, keeps the transcript so far and says so, and playback says the audio could not be read. Speaker identification receives the meeting's key only for its own pass. A microphone recording waiting to be imported is encrypted in a later step.
- **The installer's update check names IT's next step for leftover audio.** A meeting audio folder that belongs to no meeting, which the app never removes by itself, still makes a storage update wait; the reason now says IT can delete it once nothing requires keeping it.
