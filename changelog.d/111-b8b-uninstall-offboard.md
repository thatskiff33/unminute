Security hardening (#111, phase B):

- **For IT: offboarding and uninstalling in one step.** Run as the person who leaves, `uninstall.ps1 -Offboard` (with `-Silent` for Intune) first removes what Unminute keeps for them, as `--offboard` does, and only then removes the program, its Start menu shortcut, its taskbar and Start pins and its uninstall entry. If anything stops offboarding, such as a meeting, retention hold or unfinished cleanup still left, or the app still open, the uninstall stops too, says why with counts only, and keeps the program so the person can finish with it; running it again finishes the job. Other items left in Unminute's data folder, such as a workspace set aside after damage, are reported with their count and left for IT to check, and the uninstall carries on. Without `-Silent` it shows what will go and asks the person to type REMOVE. Pins to other programs are kept. See `docs/IT_DEPLOYMENT.md`, "Offboarding".
