- **Recording with the window open uses less processor time on fast screens.** The recording display now redraws about 60 times a second rather than on every refresh of the screen: on every second refresh of a 120 Hz or 144 Hz screen, and on every refresh of a 60 Hz screen, as before. Its motion is now timed rather than counted in refreshes, so it moves at the speed seen on a 120 Hz screen whatever the screen: on 120 Hz screens it moves as before, and on 60 Hz screens it now eases about twice as quickly as before. Nothing else changes in how it looks (#216).
