- **Python 3.14.10.** The app now runs on CPython 3.14.10 (was 3.14.8), an expedited security release. It makes `ssl` reject certificate chains that use the compromised eMudhra intermediate certificate authority keys (CVE-2026-115557), makes `tarfile`'s extraction filter reject links that resolve to the destination folder itself (CVE-2026-114583), and makes `http.cookiejar` ignore cookies with a negative version. It also brings 3.14.9's security fixes: a `tarfile` link-extraction fix, the bundled libexpat 2.9.0, a `tempfile.TemporaryDirectory` clean-up race that could delete files outside the folder (CVE-2026-12345), and `mimetypes` using only the registry on Windows. Other packages keep their versions.
