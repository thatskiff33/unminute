- **Draft text from both tracks uses bounded memory.** With computer audio on and the draft following both tracks, the computer audio waiting for draft text is capped at 60 seconds while draft text gets ready or catches up, whatever the audio devices' rates. At the computer audio's usual 48 kHz the live mix holds at most about 6.3 MB of computer audio in Unminute; the draft process holds at most about 1.9 MB with a 48 kHz microphone, up to 7.7 MB at 192 kHz (computed, not measured). If draft text is held up for more than 60 seconds, that first minute is still drafted from both tracks, and the audio after it is not drafted, as already happens with the microphone alone. The saved recording is never affected (#371).
