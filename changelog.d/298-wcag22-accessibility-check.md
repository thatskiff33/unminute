- **Screen readers and voice control hear the words on screen.** The playback speed button, the speaker name and "Same person as" fields in Name speakers, and the passages to leave out on the Hand off page now have accessible names that contain their visible labels (WCAG 2.2 Label in Name).
- **A stricter accessibility check before each release.** The release check of the app window now also tests target size, focus hidden behind other content, the contrast of focus rings and field borders, text spacing, keyboard traps, labels in names, image text alternatives, and the page language, title and status region, against WCAG 2.2 AA (#298).
