Security hardening (#111, slice B7a):
- **A damaged copy of a model from your IT team says so.** Where IT installed a model for everyone and its copy is found damaged, Manage downloads shows it as damaged with "Ask your IT team to repair it", rather than as installed by IT, and a meeting that stops because of it names your IT team rather than Manage downloads. A missing file, or one of the wrong size, shows at once; damage inside a file of the right size shows once a meeting fails to load the model or Check every file runs, and until then the copy shows as installed by IT. Where your own copies are allowed, the app uses yours instead, and Manage downloads also offers Repair to download your own. Once IT repairs its copy, the app shows it as ready again without a restart.
- **Clearer wording where only IT's models are used.** On a computer where your organisation uses only the models your IT team installs, a model that is not ready now says so, rather than that downloads are turned off. Where Repair is not offered, a damaged model no longer says that Repair downloads a fresh copy.
- **The model package checks itself against the installed app.** `Install-Models.ps1` refuses a package whose files differ from what an installed version of Unminute pins for the same model revision. A permission fault now fails the install or uninstall of the app or the models, rather than counting as a file in use that Intune tries again without end. `-Uninstall` removes all the models or none, and finishes when the app is already gone.
