- **Whisper on an NVIDIA graphics card.** On a computer with an NVIDIA card, Settings > Manage downloads now offers "Graphics card for Whisper", a one-time 0.57 GB download of NVIDIA's cuBLAS library for CUDA 12 from NVIDIA's own release on PyPI (files.pythonhosted.org), checked against published checksums file by file. With it downloaded, "Use the graphics card" on and Faster mode, Whisper large-v3 turbo and Whisper large-v3 transcribe on the card. On a 4 GB laptop card, a 17.5-minute public meeting took 47 s with turbo instead of 6 minutes on the processor, and 3 minutes with large-v3 instead of 31 (with the processor partly busy), and was at least as accurate against the meeting's published transcript. Large-v3 runs on the card at int8_float16, which fits in 4 GB; at float16 it did not fit. Parakeet stays on the processor, where it is faster. If the card cannot be used (an old driver, too little graphics memory or a damaged download), the rest of that transcript is made on the processor with the same model, nothing already done is lost, and the meeting says so. A meeting's Versions tab now shows where each transcript was made and at what precision, for example "Graphics card, float16" or "Processor, int8". IT can turn both graphics card components off with the existing `AllowGpuComponent` policy (#248).
