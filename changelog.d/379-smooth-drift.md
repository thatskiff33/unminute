- **Computer audio keeps time smoothly, with nothing cut or padded.** When several speakers and headsets are recorded together, each one's own clock runs slightly fast or slow. Unminute used to correct that in jumps, so in a simulation a fast device lost up to 0.1 s of its audio, and a slow one gained up to 0.1 s of silence, one to six times an hour. It now corrects each device gradually as it records, so no audio is skipped and no silence is put in (#379).
