- **A cancelled shutdown no longer skips the question when you close during a recording.** If Windows started to shut down or sign out while you were recording and the shutdown was then cancelled (by you at "these apps are preventing shutdown", or by another app), closing the window later could end the recording without asking. Closing while recording now always asks whether to stop and save, discard or keep recording. A shutdown or sign-out that goes ahead still stops and saves the recording without waiting.
