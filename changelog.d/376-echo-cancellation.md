- **Recordings with computer audio have the echo taken off the microphone.** On laptop speakers the microphone also hears the other side of a call a moment later, which gave the recording an echo when played back. When you stop, Unminute now uses the computer audio to take that echo off the microphone before mixing the two, so the recording you play back and the one that is transcribed have less echo. It is automatic, needs no setting and sends nothing anywhere; the saved tracks are only read. Importing a recording with computer audio takes a little longer. If the echo cannot be taken off, the recording is mixed as before and its report says so. Real-sound trials on laptop speakers are still to come (#376).
