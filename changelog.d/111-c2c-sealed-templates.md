Security hardening (#111, phase C):

- **Handoff templates are encrypted.** Your edited and custom templates are kept in `templates.sealed`, sealed with AES-256-GCM under the workspace's key, which Windows protects for your account on this computer. A `templates.json` from an earlier version is converted the first time you use templates, keeping its titles, prompts and versions, and is then deleted; a damaged one gives no saved templates, as before. If it cannot be read at that moment, or is unusually large (over 8 MB), it is kept as it is and tried again the next time you start Unminute, with the built-in templates meanwhile. An encrypted templates file that cannot be read leaves the built-in templates until you save one.
