Security hardening (#111, phase C):

- **Handoff templates are encrypted.** Your edited and custom templates are kept in `templates.sealed`, sealed with AES-256-GCM under the workspace's key, which Windows protects for your account on this computer. A `templates.json` from an earlier version is converted the first time you use templates, keeping its titles, prompts and versions, and is then deleted; a damaged one gives no saved templates, as before. If it cannot be read at that moment, it is kept as it is and tried again the next time you start Unminute; one that is unusually large (over 8 MB) is kept as it is and not converted, and IT can help. Until it is converted, the built-in templates are shown and templates cannot be edited or added. An encrypted templates file that is damaged leaves the built-in templates until you save one; one that cannot be read at that moment is kept, templates cannot be edited until you restart Unminute, and nothing is lost.
