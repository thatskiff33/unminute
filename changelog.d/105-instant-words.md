- **Instant words while recording (optional).** With draft text on, turn on **Show instant words** in Settings > Recording and speakers to see words in grey moments after people speak, until the draft text replaces them. They are made on this computer with NVIDIA's Nemotron Speech Streaming model, an optional download of about 0.66 GB in Manage downloads (NVIDIA Open Model License), kept in memory only, and paused while Unminute is minimised.
- **New policy: `AllowInstantWords`.** Set to 0, instant words cannot be turned on and their model cannot be downloaded in the app; they never apply when `AllowLiveDrafts` is 0. Import the administrative template again to see it.
