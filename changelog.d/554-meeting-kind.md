- **Each recording asks: in person or online.** When you select Record a meeting, the question before it starts asks which kind of meeting it is, with neither chosen: **In person meeting** records your microphone, and **Online meeting** records your microphone and the sound from this computer, including other people on the call. The text you confirm, and the statement you tick, follow your choice. **Start recording** stays unavailable until you choose, and becomes available a moment after your last choice or tick. With the keyboard, Tab to the two kinds and choose with the arrow keys or Space; each kind gives screen readers its name and what it records.
- **The computer audio switch is gone.** **Also record computer audio** is no longer in Settings or Setup: computer audio is recorded only for a recording you start as an Online meeting, so it is never left on by accident. A value saved by an earlier version is ignored. **In online meetings, draft text follows** (your microphone, or both mixed) stays in Settings > Recording and speakers and applies to online meetings only.
- **Policy decides whether Online meeting is offered.** Where `AllowComputerAudioRecording` is 0, invalid or cannot be read, the question offers only the microphone and says your organisation has turned computer audio off. Where `RequireRecordingConsent` is 0 and computer audio is allowed, a smaller window still asks In person or Online, without the statement to tick. The recording question's wording version is now 3; a recording in progress when you update keeps the confirmation it started with.
