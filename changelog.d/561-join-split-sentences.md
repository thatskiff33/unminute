- **A sentence split in two reads as one line.** When transcription splits a sentence across two passages, the transcript, copies and handoffs now show it as one line, with one time and one speaker, as long as the second part follows within a second by the same speaker. Each part is still corrected, undone, searched and played on its own; a full stop at the end of the first part, or moving one part to another speaker, shows two lines again. The saved transcript is unchanged. In Hand off, leaving out such a line leaves out both parts.
