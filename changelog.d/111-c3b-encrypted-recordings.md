Security hardening (#111, phase C):

- **Microphone recordings are now encrypted as they are saved.** Each few seconds of a recording is saved encrypted under that recording's own key, which Windows protects for your account, and no unencrypted copy is written at any point: the recording goes to the sandboxed decoder from memory, with no `recording.wav`. Once the recording has become a meeting ready to transcribe, or when you discard or delete it, its key is deleted before the saved files, so anything left on disk cannot be read. If setting up the meeting fails, the recording and its key are kept so you can choose Try again. A piece that has been damaged is left out as silence and reported, and a recording whose saved audio cannot be read at all is kept for you to delete, never removed on its own. The recording's details and your recording confirmation are kept, encrypted, with the meeting until its audio is removed.
