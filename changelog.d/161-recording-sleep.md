- **The computer stays awake while recording.** A long meeting in which nobody touches the computer no longer ends when Windows' idle sleep timer runs out, unless your organisation's power settings prevent it. Windows still decides what closing the lid does: to record with the lid closed, set it to do nothing, and stop the recording in Unminute when the meeting ends.
- **A recording stopped by sleep says so.** If the computer goes to sleep, for example because the lid was closed, the recording stops, keeps what was recorded and says the computer went to sleep, instead of reporting a lost microphone.
