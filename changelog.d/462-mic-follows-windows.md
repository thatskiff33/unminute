- **The microphone follows Windows while you record, if you started on its default.** If you started recording on the Windows default microphone and Windows starts using another one mid-recording (for example, a Bluetooth headset connects and becomes the default), the recording switches to it. A microphone you chose before pressing Record is kept. If the microphone in use is disconnected, the recording moves to the one Windows now uses instead of stopping, and follows Windows from then on unless you chose a microphone on the recording card. It carries on in the same recording: the moment of the switch is recorded as silence and listed under the card like other gaps, and the card says which microphone is recording now. A new **Microphone** list on the recording card lets you choose another microphone while recording; the recording then stays on your choice. If no microphone can be used within about 10 seconds, the recording stops and keeps what was saved, as before. Your organisation's setting for microphone recording applies to every switch. Real Bluetooth and USB headsets are still to be checked by hand (#462).
