- **The microphone follows Windows while you record.** If Windows starts using another microphone mid-recording (for example, a Bluetooth headset connects and becomes the default), the recording switches to it; if the microphone in use is disconnected, the recording moves to the one Windows now uses instead of stopping. It carries on in the same recording: the moment of the switch is recorded as silence and listed under the card like other gaps, and the card says which microphone is recording now. A new **Microphone** list on the recording card lets you choose another microphone while recording; the recording then stays on your choice. If no microphone can be used within about 10 seconds, the recording stops and keeps what was saved, as before. Your organisation's setting for microphone recording applies to every switch. Real Bluetooth and USB headsets are still to be checked by hand (#462).
