- **Record calls: computer audio beside the microphone.** Settings > Recording and speakers (and Setup) has a new switch, **Also record computer audio**, off by default. With it on, Unminute also records the sound playing on the computer, such as the other side of a Teams or Zoom call, as a second track; the two are mixed into one recording when you stop and transcribed once. Everything the computer plays is recorded, including notification sounds, so turn on Do not disturb first. The question before each recording then names people on the call, and the recording card shows a thin Computer audio bar. Your organisation can turn it off (`AllowComputerAudioRecording`). See "Recording calls" in the user guide (#108).
- **Do not go back to an earlier version of Unminute while a recording with computer audio is unfinished.** Stop the recording and let it import first: an earlier version imports only the microphone track and then deletes the recording folder, so the computer track would be lost (#108).
