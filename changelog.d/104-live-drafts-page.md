- **Draft text while recording.** Turn on "Show draft text while recording" in Settings > Recording and speakers to see rough text of what is said, a few seconds behind, on the recording card. It is made on this computer with Parakeet v2, kept in memory only, never saved, and replaced by the full transcript when you stop. Off by default; it pauses on battery unless processing on battery is allowed. Select Hide draft before sharing your screen (#104).
