- **Draft text while recording.** Turn on "Show draft text while recording" in Settings > Recording and speakers to see rough text of what is said, a few seconds behind, on the recording card. A stretch of talk with no pause shows in pieces of up to about 10 seconds, a length chosen after trials of 6, 8 and 10 seconds. It is made on this computer with Parakeet v2, kept in memory only, never saved, and replaced by the full transcript when you stop. Off by default; it pauses on battery unless processing on battery is allowed. Select Hide draft before sharing your screen (#104).
