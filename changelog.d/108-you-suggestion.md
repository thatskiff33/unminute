- **Name speakers suggests "You" for a call recorded with computer audio.** The speaker who was mostly on your microphone, not the other side heard through your speakers, is marked "Mostly on your microphone: probably you." Select **Use 'You'** to name them, or type another name; nothing is named until you choose. Unminute keeps a small encrypted record of which side was speaking when, and removes it with the meeting's audio.
- **A recovered recording says how much of each track was saved.** After the app closes unexpectedly while recording with computer audio, the recovered recording shows the microphone's and the computer audio's saved length.
