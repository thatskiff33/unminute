The reviewed text is saved in the workspace first. Then the app's copy of the audio is removed. After this, playback and transcribing again are not possible and the text can no longer be edited.

This removes the app's own working copies on this computer. It cannot remove copies elsewhere: synced or backed-up folders (for example OneDrive), the original on a phone or recorder, or anything you shared with an AI assistant or other service. Ordinary deletion is not a forensic erasure of the disk.
